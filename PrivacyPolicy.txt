BaseJumper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the BaseJumper application ("the Game") provided for Windows desktop devices. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent, secure gaming experience.

1. Information Collection and Use

BaseJumper is a standalone, mathematically procedural application built entirely in custom C++. We respect your privacy and have designed the Game to operate entirely offline without the need for data collection.

    No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, or locations.

    No Telemetry: We do not track your gameplay data, crash reports, device hardware specifications, or usage metrics.

    Local Storage Only: Any data generated by the Game (such as high scores or settings) is saved strictly locally on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

The Game does not utilize any third-party analytics software, advertising frameworks, or background trackers. Because BaseJumper does not connect to the internet, no data is ever shared with or sold to third-party entities.

3. Children's Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information from any user, our application is completely safe and compliant with privacy regulations regarding children under the age of 13 (such as COPPA).

4. Changes and Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, though changes will be rare given the offline nature of the Game. Any changes will be reflected by the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub.
